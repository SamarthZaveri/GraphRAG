Local search walks 2 hops of the graph plus R-GCN-nearest entities, grounded in the original source text. Global search reads the community summaries most relevant to your question. On Auto, a trained contextual bandit picks GraphRAG or vector RAG per question — based on what kind of question it is and how the corpus is structured — rather than one fixed choice for the whole corpus. The benchmark scores both against a vanilla vector-RAG baseline. Runs fully offline via a local Ollama model — no API key, nothing leaves your machine.
